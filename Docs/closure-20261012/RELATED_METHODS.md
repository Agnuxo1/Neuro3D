# Bounded comparison of primary methods

Read on 12 October 2026: the sections identified below. This targeted comparison
does not complete every historical literature extraction or demonstrate novelty.
No third-party implementation is copied into Neuro3D in this campaign.

| Primary method and sections read | Shared mechanism | Difference from the captured Neuro3D model | Remaining comparison |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Dr.Jit, 2022](https://arxiv.org/html/2202.01284), introduction and automatic differentiation | Scene-dependent geometric computation, differentiated transport and compilation | Physically based image rendering/Monte Carlo and AD versus represented coherent scalar fields plus exact-family/decision enclosures | Actual matched numerical and total-cost benchmark; the authors' speedups do not transfer to Neuro3D |
| [Clements et al.](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1603.08788), Background and Decomposition method | Complex linear interference and trainable phases | Universal ideal unitary mesh versus the operator set reachable by a particular captured 104-object geometry | Verify actual F_geom reachable dimension; auxiliary 64-mode mesh remains separate |
| [Pai et al.](https://arxiv.org/html/2205.08501), Methods, circuit packaging and Appendices F/H | Coherent forward/adjoint fields and phase derivatives | Experimental photonic mesh with calibrated electronics and digital nonlinearities versus software/hybrid geometry training | Match observables, gradient error and complete cost; no physical claim from software shader validation |
| [Hamerly et al.](https://arxiv.org/html/1812.07614), architecture and energy discussion | Optical linear multiplication, photon/noise accounting | Photoelectric homodyne architecture and scale arguments versus GPU simulation of represented fields | Full host/modulation/detection cost; reference photon scales do not measure a Neuro3D device |
| [Wang et al.](https://arxiv.org/html/2104.13467), experiment and detection discussion | Accuracy in a photon-limited optical calculation | Experimentally measured optical multiplication versus electronic computation of an optical model | A comparable real physical measurement is absent |
| [Wright et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2104.13386), abstract/identity only | Hybrid physical/digital training | Actual controllable physical systems versus represented Blender geometry | Full methods/supplement reading still required; DOI resolution alone is not full-paper review |

Candidate contribution remains falsifiable: useful joint certification of
represented geometry, coherent transport and decisions at a measured proof
cost in a declared family. Matched comparison can refute that utility. These
method differences are an inference from the stated model contracts, not a
claim of absent prior work, a new physical principle or exceptional importance.
