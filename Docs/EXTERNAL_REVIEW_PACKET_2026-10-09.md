## Questions that can invalidate or limit the claims

| Question for the reviewer | Required evidence or rejection condition |
|---|---|
| Does the admitted graph cover the declared optical model? | Rebuild captured evaluated meshes; check nearest surfaces, closed fans, every nonzero branch, roots/ports and topology. Reject hidden CPU replacement of wrong GPU candidates. |
| Are channels actually coherent? | Reproduce global-phase invariance and relative-phase interference changes. Independent-source intensity summation is a different function. |
| Is the certificate about the intended physical system? | Identify represented input/geometry, observed native arithmetic and absent transform/measurement/model preimages. Do not extrapolate the current certificate to all gradients or physical flux. |
| Does learning generalize? | Reproduce train-only preprocessing and all fixed seeds/baselines. Iris had already been used; Wine results do not demonstrate superiority. New blind data and preregistered comparisons are required. |
| Does GPU rendering accelerate this network? | Compare the same outputs/precision and total cost against own geometryDAG and appropriate algebraic baselines. Current raster/deferred and OptiX costs include negative outcomes. A high utilization display is not a speed or energy result. |
| Is the whole network physically faithful? | Derive finite-aperture/vector/wave effects and calibrated coupler/detector models. The standalone Gaussian reference and algebraic isometry do not provide this validation. |
| Is the contribution scientifically original? | Compare closest ray/diffraction differentiable optics and certification methods under equivalent contracts. Original implementation/integration does not itself establish exceptional scientific importance. |
| Is recovery durable? | Repeat interrupted-resume and electrical-failure conditions independently; current exact owner-loss recovery covers a narrower admitted sequence. |
